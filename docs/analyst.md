# Using the analyst workspace

**Explore** searches reported dimensional facts from published filings. The explorer reads the latest 12 published filings through the selected date. Choose an earlier date to research an older window. Select a breakdown identified by concept, unit, axis, all other dimensions, and reporting period. Hold Ctrl or Command in the member selector to chart and export a subset. Search results show up to 200 breakdowns. Exact taxonomy names keep definition changes visible as separate series. Six- and nine-month cumulative disclosures have independent histories.

A share divides a member by its reported total in the same dates, unit and fixed dimensions. A subtotal can include eliminations or overlapping members. The table preserves each reported member. Growth uses the change divided by the absolute prior value with matching year-over-year periods. Source links in the table and CSV identify the filings behind each calculation.

**Compare** accepts an anchor and up to four other imported tickers. Monetary revenues have a separate column per currency. Cash conversion uses operating cash flow divided by net income attributable to the parent. Capital intensity uses reported PP&E cash purchases divided by revenue. Annual trends use each issuer's actual fiscal dates.

**Model** starts with a selected annual revenue baseline. Enter percentage points (enter 10 for 10%). Opening working capital is a percentage of baseline sales. Each forecast year applies growth to prior sales and applies the operating margin, D&A, capex and working capital ratios to its projected sales. Taxes equal projected operating income times the assumed tax rate, including the implied benefit on a loss. Free cash flow to the firm equals operating income less taxes, plus D&A, less capex and the increase in non-cash working capital. Year-end discounting produces an enterprise value at the baseline date.

The terminal calculation grows the fifth year's FCFF by the terminal growth rate and divides by WACC less growth. The grid moves WACC in one percentage-point steps and terminal growth in half-point steps. A missing driver leaves dependent calculations blank. Set explicit zeros for any assumed zero flows. Save the calculated scenarios under a name to reopen on another signed-in device. A saved model retains its baseline, filing sources, assumptions, outputs and calculation version. Use **Rebase on selected filing** and evaluate again to apply revised historical data. Saved explorers and peer sets reopen their filters over current published filings.
